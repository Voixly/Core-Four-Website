  <h2> What is Standing Seam Metal Roofing?
  <p> When homeowners want a roof that they will likely never have to replace again, they turn to standing seam metal roofing. Unlike the exposed-fastener corrugated metal panels often seen on barns or sheds, a standing seam system is a premium architectural product. It consists of continuous metal panels (typically steel or aluminum) that run vertically from the ridge of the roof down to the eaves. The defining feature of a standing seam roof is the raised, interlocking seams that connect each panel.
  <p> These seams are elevated above the level of the roof deck, and all the fasteners are completely hidden underneath them.This concealed fastener design is crucial—it completely eliminates the risk of water penetrating through screw holes, which is the most common cause of leaks in cheaper metal roofs. The result is a sleek, modern aesthetic combined with a virtually impenetrable, weatherproof barrier that is tailor-made to handle the extreme weather volatility of Texas.
