  <h2.title> Frequently Asked Questions About Roofing Claims
  <div.desc> Find answers to the most common questions homeowners and property managers have about the insurance claim process.
  <div.title> Will my rates go up if I file a roof claim?
  <span.num> 1
  [ICON] icon-plus acc-icon-plus
  [ICON] icon-minus acc-icon-minus
  <div.answer> In Texas, insurance companies generally cannot raise your individual premium solely because you filed a claim for an 'Act of God' like a hail or wind storm. However, if a massive storm hits your entire zip code, base rates for the whole area may increase regardless of whether you file a claim or not.
  <div.title> Can a roofer waive or 'eat' my deductible?
  <span.num> 2
  [ICON] icon-plus acc-icon-plus
  [ICON] icon-minus acc-icon-minus
  <div.answer> No. Under Texas law (HB2102), it is a criminal offense for a roofing contractor to pay, waive, absorb, or rebate a homeowner's insurance deductible. Any roofer offering to do so is committing insurance fraud. However, Core Four Roofing offers flexible financing options to help you cover the cost of your deductible comfortably.
  <div.title> How long do I have to file a claim after a storm?
  <span.num> 3
  [ICON] icon-plus acc-icon-plus
  [ICON] icon-minus acc-icon-minus
  <div.answer> Most insurance policies require you to file a claim within one year (365 days) of the date of the storm. However, it is critical to get your roof inspected immediately, as waiting can cause secondary water damage to your home's interior, which may not be covered if deemed negligent.
  <div.title> My insurance only approved a partial repair. What do I do?
  <span.num> 4
  [ICON] icon-plus acc-icon-plus
  [ICON] icon-minus acc-icon-minus
  <div.answer> Call us immediately. Insurance companies often try to minimize payouts by suggesting a repair instead of a replacement. If your roof has sustained widespread damage or if the shingles are too old and brittle to be successfully repaired without causing more damage, we will document this and supplement the claim for a full replacement.
