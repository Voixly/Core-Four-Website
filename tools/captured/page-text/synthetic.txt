=== SECTION 1 | h=1100 | pad=96px/96px | bg=rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)
  <h1> What Are F-Wave™ Synthetic Shingles?
  <p> For decades, roofing materials have had the same fundamental weaknesses: asphalt shingles lose their granules, wood shake rots and splits, and real slate is heavy and fragile. F-Wave™ synthetic roofing eliminates all of these problems through modern engineering. Made from a proprietary, commercial-grade polymer mix, F-Wave™ shingles contain absolutely no asphalt, fiberglass, or crushed stone granules.
  <p> Instead, they are manufactured as a single-piece, highly durable synthetic material. This means there is nothing to delaminate, nothing to wash away into your gutters, and nothing to crack under the impact of massive hail. Designed to mimic the exact textures and dimensional shadow lines of premium architectural shingles, hand-split wood shake, or natural quarried slate, F-Wave provides Texas homeowners with a luxurious aesthetic that is virtually indestructible.
