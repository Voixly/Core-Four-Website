=== SECTION 1 | h=985 | pad=96px/96px | bg=rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)
  <h2> What Are Architectural Asphalt Shingles?
  <p> Asphalt shingles have been the leading choice for residential roofing for decades, but modern technology has vastly improved their performance. While traditional "3-tab" shingles lay flat, the architectural asphalt shingles (also known as dimensional shingles) that Core Four Roofing installs are constructed with multiple layers of fiberglass and asphalt. This multi-layered design creates a dimensional, textured appearance that mimics the look of wood shake or slate.
  <p> More importantly, this added thickness provides superior protection against the harsh Texas climate. Premium architectural shingles feature advanced sealant bands for high wind resistance, algae-resistant granules to prevent black streaking, and enhanced impact resistance to defend against hail. Whether you are replacing an aging roof or recovering from storm damage, asphalt shingles offer the perfect balance of durability, aesthetic appeal, and affordability for your home.

  <h2.title> Frequently Asked Questions About Asphalt Shingle Roofs
  <div.title> How long does an asphalt shingle roof last in Texas?
  <span.num> 1
  [ICON] icon-plus acc-icon-plus
  [ICON] icon-minus acc-icon-minus
  <div.answer> In the demanding Texas climate, a properly installed architectural asphalt shingle roof typically lasts between 20 to 30 years. Regular maintenance, proper attic ventilation, and prompt storm damage repairs can help maximize the lifespan of your shingles.
  <div.title> What is the difference between 3-tab and architectural shingles?
  <span.num> 2
  [ICON] icon-plus acc-icon-plus
  [ICON] icon-minus acc-icon-minus
  <div.answer> 3-tab shingles are a single, flat layer of material, offering basic protection and a lifespan of about 15-20 years. Architectural shingles are multi-layered, providing a thicker, three-dimensional look, significantly higher wind and hail resistance, and longer lifespans. Core Four Roofing highly recommends architectural shingles for Texas homes.
  <div.title> Are asphalt shingles impact resistant against hail?
  <span.num> 3
  [ICON] icon-plus acc-icon-plus
  [ICON] icon-minus acc-icon-minus
  <div.answer> While standard architectural shingles offer good protection, Core Four Roofing also installs specialized Class 3 and Class 4 impact-resistant asphalt shingles (such as those from Malarkey). These are formulated with polymer-modified asphalt to absorb hail impacts, often qualifying you for a discount on your homeowner's insurance premiums.
  <div.title> Will my new shingle roof get those ugly black streaks over time?
  <span.num> 4
  [ICON] icon-plus acc-icon-plus
  [ICON] icon-minus acc-icon-minus
  <div.answer> We install premium shingles that feature copper-lined granules specifically designed to prevent the growth of blue-green algae, which is the primary cause of those unsightly black streaks in humid environments like Houston.
